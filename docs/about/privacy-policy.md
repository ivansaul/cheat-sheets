# Privacy Policy

Cheat Sheets ("we", "our", or "us") operates as an open-source, client-side application. This page informs users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information.

By choosing to use Cheat Sheets, you agree to the practices in relation to this policy.

## Information Collection and Use

Cheat Sheets inherently safeguards your privacy by not collecting your data. 

- **No Personal Data:** We do not collect, store, transmit, or process any personally identifiable information (PII) or sensitive user data.
- **Local Storage:** The application generates and stores user preferences (e.g., app theme, language) locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any external server.
- **Third-Party Services:** We do not employ third-party services, tracking SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks, or remote crash reporting services.

## Log Data and Cookies

Since Cheat Sheets does not require user authentication or track usage statistics, we **do not** collect Log Data (such as your device IP address, device name, or operating system version) and we **do not** use Cookies.

## Security

We value your trust. Because no user data is collected or transmitted, Cheat Sheets minimizes privacy and security risks by design. Furthermore, as an open-source application, the complete [source code] is publicly available for anyone to audit and verify our data collection claims independently. 
  
  [source code]: https://github.com/ivansaul/cheat-sheets

## Children’s Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. Because our application does not collect any personal data whatsoever, no child's data is at risk.
